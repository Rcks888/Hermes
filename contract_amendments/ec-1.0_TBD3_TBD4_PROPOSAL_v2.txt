2. TBD-3: spread-model source and n (C3.2)
------------------------------------------
S1 [approved, unchanged] Source: the 0g-1 decision-time quotes of live cycles.
S2 [approved, unchanged] Window: every cycle from the first 0g-1 cycle
   (7 Oct 2026) to Sun 22 Nov 2026 23:59:59 UTC, both accounts (S6).

S3 v2 Selection, in this order, deterministic:
   (1) Sequence. Take every event of logs/events.jsonl whose `event` is in
       CYCLE_EVENTS (NO_SIGNAL, SIGNAL_APPROVED, SIGNAL_REJECTED,
       CYCLE_ABORTED), from the first 0g-1 cycle to the end of the
       population's window, ordered by `ts` with ties in file line order
       (the stable sort stale_flags already uses).
   (2) Staleness. stale_flags is applied once to that whole sequence, so each
       cycle's flags are those of the full log, not of a week.
   (3) First occurrence. For each bar_time the first cycle in the sequence is
       that bar's cycle. Every later cycle with the same bar_time is
       discarded, whatever its content. The first cycle is never replaced by a
       later rerun, even when the first fails (4).
   (4) Inclusion, on the first cycle only: event NO_SIGNAL, SIGNAL_APPROVED or
       SIGNAL_REJECTED; `quote` present with quote_error null; stale_flags
       empty; no scheduling_delay_note; and |live D - replay E| <= 60 s (S4,
       the E1 limit). A bar whose first cycle fails contributes no point.
   (5) Window. The cycle's `ts` (UTC log time) decides the window: calibration
       is ts <= 2026-11-22T23:59:59Z (S2); the forward population is
       ts >= 2026-11-23T00:00:00Z (T3).
   Every exclusion is counted by reason and session in the artefact.

S4 v2 Points and session:
   - points = (Decimal(str(quote_ask)) - Decimal(str(quote_bid))) / Decimal(str(point)),
     point 0.01 from the broker spec. Quotes carry 2 digits, so the result is
     an integer and NO rounding is applied. A cycle where it is not an exact
     integer, or is below 1, is excluded and counted (expected 0). The count
     of cycles whose quote_spread_price / point differs from it is reported
     (expected 0).
   - Session = clock.session_of(E), E = clock.entry_instant(clock.
     decision_instant(bar_open, 480)), bar_open from bar_time (the server
     clock, conformance P2). This is the session the C3.3 draw uses (step 5)
     at the baseline delay.
   - Alignment shown, not assumed: for every included point the artefact
     reports live D - E, with live D = bar_close + scheduling_delay_seconds
     (E1, the same convention), and the count of points whose logged session
     (session of live D) differs from the session of E. Expected 0: E is
     bar close + 480 s, so E and D lie in minutes :08, :23, :38 or :53 and
     a gap of at most 60 s cannot cross an hour. Any nonzero count is
     reported; the point keeps the session of E.

S5 v2 Sufficiency: the 0g-1 source is used iff every session has at least
   ASIA 437, LONDON 278, NY 447, OFF 44 points under S3/S4 (fixed constants,
   Q2). Otherwise the model is the soak for ALL four sessions: each
   logs/soak.json cycle with a spread, its integer points as logged, its
   session reclassified as engine get_session_tag(UTC hour of its timestamp),
   which is how C2.2 classifies. The probe-local session field is not used.
   Sources are never mixed per session. The artefact records both
   classifications' counts for the soak, for information.

S6 [approved conditionally; evidence attached] Accounts are not a model
   boundary. Bundle file BROKER_SPEC_COMPARISON.txt: the 34 fields of
   bs-39eef15e09e9 (5 Nov, demo 10013386522) and bs-596c87c5a2bb (16 Nov,
   demo 115681802) are equal except margin_probe.price and
   margin_probe.margin, a probe taken at the price of the day (4124.57 and
   3859.85); margin / price is 1.000 in both. Both JSON files are attached.
   The artefact records the per-account n and per-session median, for
   information only.

Artefact: research/data_feasibility/spread_model_<UTC stamp>.json with
{"source", "window", "inclusion_rule": "TBD-3 S1-S6 v2", "n_by_session",
"exclusions" (by reason and session), "alignment" (D - E summary, session
mismatch count), "by_session": sorted points, "sha256"}. The commit that adds
it freezes TBD-3, and its hash enters the run identity (C6.1).


3. TBD-4: E2 price tolerance (section 8)
----------------------------------------
T1 [approved, unchanged] Calibration set: every cycle meeting S3 in the S2
   window, with a replay bid at E in the new export. A cycle with no M1 bar
   at E is counted and reported, not used.
T2 [approved as a calibration rule, unchanged] T = type-1 99th percentile of
   |replay bid - live quote_bid| over the calibration set, rounded up to the
   next 0.01. Frozen with its n, the export id and a sha256 of the
   (bar_time, replay_bid, live_bid) rows. The calibration exceedance rate is
   reported as description only (Q4).

T3 v2 Breach, two conditions on two populations:
   (a) Signal condition. Population: EVERY E0-eligible signal cycle (section 8
       E0), with no calibration exclusion applied: stale ticks, suspect
       delays and reruns are all included. Breach if any has
       |replay bid - live quote_bid| > 2T, or has no replay bid at E.
   (b) Rate condition. Population: the FORWARD population, cycles selected
       by S3 with ts >= 2026-11-23T00:00:00Z, up to the conformance cutoff,
       each with a replay bid at E (those without are counted, not used).
       If it holds fewer than 400 cycles, E2 is NOT ASSESSED. Otherwise
       breach if more than 2% of it has |diff| > T.
   Differences and T are compared in integer cents. 2T, 2% and 400 are
   declared engineering tolerances, not confidence bounds (Q5). 400 is about
   one week of forward cycles after S3 (480 slots less stale and suspect
   ones), so E2 is assessable no earlier than about 30 Nov.
T4 [approved, unchanged] Reported, never a breach: signed median and
   p10/p50/p90 of the difference by session, missing-M1 counts, account split.
T5 v2 This changes an acceptance rule, so it is proposed as an explicit
   section 8 amendment (text in section 4). It is implemented now, for
   review, in replay/conformance.py: e2(signal_pairs, forward_pairs,
   tolerance) and verdict(). Before TBD-4 is frozen (tolerance None) E2 is
   reported only, as ec-0.3 says. Tests (tests/test_conformance.py,
   test_e2_price and test_levels_and_verdict) and their controls: signal
   condition at T instead of 2T; rate 3% instead of 2%; no forward minimum;
   missing replay bid ignored; "not assessed" accepted by the verdict. Each
   control fails on its own assertion.
