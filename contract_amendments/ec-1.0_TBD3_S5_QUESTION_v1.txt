3. Question
-----------
Q-S5. Which soak population does S5 v2 mean?
  A  [recommended] The 0a soak as measured on 30 Sep: the 1,341 cycles above,
     identified by the copy's sha256 and the cut-off timestamp. It is the
     population v2 named and the reconciliation measured, it is fixed, and
     it lies wholly before the 0g-1 window.
  B  Every soak cycle with a spread to 2026-11-22T23:59:59Z (the S2 end),
     from all copies, de-duplicated by timestamp. Larger, but it is a
     population v2 did not describe, it overlaps the 0g-1 window and both
     accounts, and it needs a new de-duplication rule.
  C  Another rule the Reviewer sets.

Under A or B the build would no longer read logs/soak.json. Proposed
implementation (no rule change): the chosen source file(s) are copied into
research/data_feasibility/ and committed with the artefacts; the build takes
them by path, refuses unless each sha256 matches the value recorded here,
applies the cut-off, and records file, sha256, cut-off and n in the artefact.
A soak cycle holds no credential or account detail (timestamp, session,
connection flags, XAUUSD symbol_info, spread, offset, resources, error text).
