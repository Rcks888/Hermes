2. TBD-3: spread-model source and n (C3.2)
------------------------------------------
Proposed rule (S1-S6):

S1 Source: the 0g-1 decision-time `quote_spread_price` of live cycles, as the
   contract prefers, because it is measured at the instant the replay draws
   for (C3.3 uses the spread at E, at most 60 s after the live D, E1).
S2 Window: every cycle from the first 0g-1 cycle (7 Oct 2026) to Sun 22 Nov
   2026 23:59 UTC, both accounts (S6).
S3 Inclusion: event NO_SIGNAL, SIGNAL_APPROVED or SIGNAL_REJECTED, with
   quote_error null, a fresh tick (stale_flags empty) and no
   scheduling_delay_note. One point per (bar_time, session): a repeated bar
   (manual rerun) keeps the first logged cycle.
S4 Session: the event's logged `session` (session of D). Points are integer
   points: round(quote_spread_price / point), point 0.01 from the broker spec.
S5 Sufficiency: the 0g-1 source is used iff every session has at least the
   soak's n for that session (ASIA 437, LONDON 278, NY 447, OFF 44). If any
   session falls short, the model is the 1,206 soak measurements for ALL four
   sessions. Sources are never mixed per session, so the artefact has one
   provenance.
S6 Accounts: the account change is not a model boundary, because the server,
   symbol spec and feed are unchanged. The artefact records the per-account n
   and the two-sample median per session, for information only.

Artefact: research/data_feasibility/spread_model_<UTC stamp>.json with
{"source", "window", "inclusion_rule": "TBD-3 S1-S6 v1", "n_by_session",
"by_session": sorted points, "sha256"}. replay/spread.SpreadModel.load
already checks sha256 against the canonical hash. The commit that adds it
freezes TBD-3, and its hash goes into the run identity (C6.1).

Questions for the Reviewer:
- Q1 S3 excludes stale-tick cycles. They are the quotes live would have used,
  but a stale tick's spread is not a current market spread. Exclude (proposed)
  or include?
- Q2 S5 uses the soak n as the minimum. Is a fixed n (for example 200 for each
  session, 40 for OFF) preferred?
- Q3 S4 uses the session of D. C3.3 step 5 draws from the session of E. They
  differ only for bars where E crosses a session boundary (at most 60 s);
  accept, or reclassify points by the session of the quote time?


3. TBD-4: E2 price tolerance (section 8)
----------------------------------------
What E2 compares: the replay bid at E (open of the M1 bar stamped E, C1.6)
against live quote_bid. At the baseline delay E is bar close + 480 s; the
live quote is taken about 5 s later (delay p50 484 s), so a difference holds
tick movement within those seconds as well as any feed difference. The
first point (7 Oct) is 0.18.

Proposed rule (T1-T5):

T1 Calibration set: every cycle meeting S3 in the S2 window (signal or not),
   with a replay bid at E in the new export. A cycle with no M1 bar at E is
   counted and reported, not used.
T2 Tolerance: T = type-1 99th percentile of |replay bid - live quote_bid| over
   the calibration set, rounded up to the next 0.01. Frozen with its n, the
   export id and a sha256 of the (bar_time, replay_bid, live_bid) rows.
T3 Breach: the replay is in E2 breach iff either
   (a) any E0-eligible signal cycle has |diff| > 2T, or
   (b) in the conformance report's cycles (every cycle meeting S3), more than
       2% have |diff| > T (twice the calibration rate by construction).
T4 Reported, never a breach: the signed median and p10/p50/p90 of the
   difference by session, the missing-M1 count, and the account split.
T5 conformance.py implements T3 as two checks. The present e2() implements a
   per-point tolerance only and gets the 2% rule when TBD-4 is frozen; its test
   then changes with it and its control is redone.

Questions for the Reviewer:
- Q4 The calibration and conformance windows overlap (both are live cycles).
  No strategy outcome enters T, but the conformance rule (b) is partly
  self-referential for cycles before 23 Nov. Accept, or calibrate on
  7 Oct-22 Nov and judge (b) only on cycles from 23 Nov?
- Q5 2T in T3(a) and 2% in T3(b) are judgement, not derived. Alternatives:
  T3(a) at T, or a binomial bound for (b).
